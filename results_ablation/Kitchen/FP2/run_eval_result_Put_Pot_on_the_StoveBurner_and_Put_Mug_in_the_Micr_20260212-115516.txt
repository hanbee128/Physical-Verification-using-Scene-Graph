Task: Put Pot on the StoveBurner and Put Mug in the Microwave
================================================================================
Here is the Python-like program for the task:

```
def put_pot_on_stoveburner_and_mug_in_microwave():
    # Step 1: Find the pot
    assert('Pot' visible)
        else: GoToObject('Pot')
    GoToObject('Pot')
    assert('close' to 'Pot')
        else: GoToObject('Pot')
    PickupObject('Pot')

    # Step 2: Move to the stove and put the pot on the burner
    GoToObject('StoveBurner')
    assert('close' to 'StoveBurner')
        else: GoToObject('StoveBurner')
    PutObject('Pot', 'StoveBurner')

    # Step 3: Find the mug
    assert('Mug' visible)
        else: GoToObject('Mug')
    GoToObject('Mug')
    assert('close' to 'Mug')
        else: GoToObject('Mug')
    PickupObject('Mug')

    # Step 4: Move to the microwave and put the mug inside
    GoToObject('Microwave')
    assert('close' to 'Microwave')
        else: GoToObject('Microwave')
    PutObject('Mug', 'Microwave')
```
