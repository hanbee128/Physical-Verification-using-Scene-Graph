Task: Put Plunger in the cabinet and Put the PaperTowelRoll on the countertop
================================================================================
Here is the Python-like program for the task:

```
def put_plunger_and_papertowello_roll():
    # Step 1: Find the Plunger
    assert('Plunger' visible)
        else: GoToObject('Plunger')
    GoToObject('Plunger')
    assert('close' to 'Plunger')
        else: GoToObject('Plunger')
    PickupObject('Plunger')

    # Step 2: Move to the cabinet and open it
    GoToObject('Cabinet')
    assert('close' to 'Cabinet')
        else: GoToObject('Cabinet')
    OpenObject('Cabinet')

    # Step 3: Put the Plunger inside the cabinet
    assert('Plunger' in 'hands')
        else: GoToObject('Plunger')
        else: PickupObject('Plunger')
    PutObject('Plunger', 'Cabinet')
    CloseObject('Cabinet')

    # Step 4: Find the PaperTowelRoll
    assert('PaperTowelRoll' visible)
        else: GoToObject('PaperTowelRoll')
    GoToObject('PaperTowelRoll')
    assert('close' to 'PaperTowelRoll')
        else: GoToObject('PaperTowelRoll')

    # Step 5: Put the PaperTowelRoll on the countertop
    PickupObject('PaperTowelRoll')
    GoToObject('CounterTop')
    PutObject('PaperTowelRoll', 'CounterTop')
```
